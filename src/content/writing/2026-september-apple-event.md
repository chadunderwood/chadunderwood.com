Battery life is incredible.

Dual neural engine is a nice added feature.

Overall an incremental update.

## Apple Watch Series 12
Health sensing is a cool feature but not an upgrade for me. However the ceramic being brought back is a highlight. Not sure if that's enough to make me leave behind my Ultra 2

## Apple AirPods
Another incremental upgrade. Will be a great addition for those needing new AirPods but for Pro users no change.

## iPhone Duo
The screen image transition from folded to unfolded is amazing. So much detail put into that one piece.

I can already see how the camera functions will help my wife take pictures of our kids easier.

The FaceTime features will be greatly used with the way my kids like to run while we are on FaceTimes with family.
