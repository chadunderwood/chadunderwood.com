Benji Taylor wrote that a personal website should feel like you've briefly left the rest of the internet. That line stuck.

This site leans into that: dark, calm, conversational bio on the home page, a macOS-style dock instead of a mega-nav, writing without card grids or engagement bait.

### What this post is

An **example** essay (see the badge on the index). It also demonstrates **editions** — this is v2, with an updated date after a light rewrite.
